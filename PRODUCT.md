# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FitCore is a personal fitness and nutrition app for an active individual who tracks food, training, weight, and diet adherence throughout the day. The primary job is to make daily decisions quickly, understand progress at a glance, and stay consistent without needing a separate coach or spreadsheet.

## Product Purpose

FitCore turns personal measurements, food logs, exercise, and diet protocols into practical daily guidance. Success means the user can immediately understand what to do next, trust the calculations, and maintain a sustainable routine across nutrition and training.

## Brand Personality

Focused, athletic, data-driven. The experience should combine Whoop's clarity and performance feedback with Nike Training Club's confident coaching tone.

## Anti-references

Avoid pastel wellness styling, playful lifestyle-app decoration, generic card-heavy dashboards, excessive gradients, and visual effects that compete with the user's data.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with today's most important state and next action.
2. Make performance data legible before making it decorative.
3. Reveal advanced controls where they are relevant to the active protocol.
4. Use confident coaching language without overstating certainty.
5. Keep repeated daily interactions fast, familiar, and responsive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, provide visible keyboard focus, maintain touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels, avoid relying on color alone, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
