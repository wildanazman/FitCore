# Home polish direction

Preserve the blue/white/gold FitCore identity and the existing logging flows. Make the daily dashboard feel like a well-made training companion, with useful workout detail and distinct material treatments.

Palette: porcelain #f4f6fa, paper #ffffff, ink #17253a, training blue #2453ee, gold #eab845, readable gold #78570d. Use the existing semantic theme tokens so other themes remain supported.

Type: Hanken Grotesk throughout. Greeting 30px, energy reading 54px, section headings 23px, workout heading 20px, body 13–14px. Keep readable labels and tabular numerals.

Layout: left-aligned greeting; a contained weekly check-in; compact weight entry; blue energy card with gold readings and its segmented meter; paired logging actions; workout card with the next actual exercise; white macro panel; daily journal.

```
Brand                       Profile
Greeting                    Goal
┌ Weekly check-in + dates ────────┐
└ Date controls ─────────────────┘
Weight check-in
╔ Blue calorie card + gold meter ╗
╚ Eaten                 Target ══╝
Log meal              Log activity
Workout / next exercise / View plan
Macros / rings / helpful food ideas
Today's log / checklist
```

Calibration: avoid giving every section the same rounded-card treatment. The energy card carries depth and the athletic segmented meter; the week is a crisp calendar; the workout card exposes real prescriptions; secondary sections use quieter surfaces. No new marketing copy, fake progress, coral, photographic filler or automatic decorative animation.
