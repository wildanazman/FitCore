## Direction contract

THESIS: Build a personal first day through responsive decisions, not a mandatory sports/diet questionnaire.

OWN-WORLD: Established typography, cobalt selection, chalk ground, precise measuring controls and flat preview rows; no emojis, characters or invented health outcomes.

STORY: Choose a goal; confirm age, equation sex, height and weight one at a time; choose everyday movement; compare safe strategies; review a named first-day preview. Sports and advanced protocols stay optional after setup.

User clarification: losing fat and building muscle are compatible intents. Weight direction is Lose fat / Maintain / Gain weight; an independent “Build muscle too” toggle persists an optional strength preference without changing the weight goal or calorie strategy. No running plan or completed workout is created.

Estimates are not exact measurements or guarantees.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo, meaningful chapter progress, confident goal prompt and large choice rows. The selected goal becomes a persistent chip. Body inputs use a direct numeric field plus a functioning graduated range ruler and increment controls.
