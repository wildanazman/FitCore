# Show Up streak

Mode: Operate. Local extension of Home's approved daily ledger, not a replacement identity. User approved meal/activity/weight check-ins, milestones, a seven-day trail and one rescue day per week. No emojis, health-pressure rewards or push to main.

Header consistency addition: all standard pages share Home's FitCore brand/date/profile header and the same responsive Hanken heading scale, supporting copy and spacing. Achievements must read as part of FitCore, not a separate app. Camera capture retains its task-specific toolbar.

Welcoming revision: replace the small underlined date disclosure with a clear calendar row and previous/next-week controls, retaining historical-date selection and disabled future dates. Achievements uses a featured earned badge (or an honest first-win empty state), a visual two-column badge collection and actionable next milestone. Celebrate actual check-ins, never invented awards. Shared header stays unchanged; no emoji, sound or decorative looping animation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make returning feel worthwhile, not compulsory. Reward recording a real day, never restriction or exercise volume.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit chalk, cobalt, navy and Hanken Grotesk. Seven selectable date stamps carry checked, rescued and pending states with icons plus text.

STORY: See your chain, make one real log, get a stamp, and discover the next milestone. A missed day is not a moral failure.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Replace the existing Home calendar with a compact streak/date workspace beneath the greeting. Monday–Sunday day stamps (44px minimum, locally scrollable on narrow screens) show the selected calendar week; future dates are disabled. Today's next action follows. Achievements live at /achievements, linked from Home, not in an expanded Home list. Fuel stays directly beneath.

FORM: Precisely approved local extension; no new-world seed. Code-led. Signature: a milestone popup uses a badge stamp, one bounded 24-piece confetti burst and expanding circle. No sounds, loops or new libraries. The modal is dismissible immediately, traps/restores focus and honors reduced motion. Seen milestones persist with app data and never repeatedly pop up. Normal check-ins keep a brief inline stamp. Historical logs recalculate the trail; rescued dates preserve continuity but never add check-ins.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
