# Home

Mode: Operate. The user delegated visual direction and requested an ambitious homepage with working animations, strong functionality and no emojis.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A vivid daily ledger that puts food, movement and habits into one readable day. Replace the competing training-first brief and repeated calorie summaries.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk-white ground, electric cobalt energy field, deep navy type, Hanken Grotesk lettering and a citrus measurement marker. Flat surfaces, defined controls and generous section separation.

STORY: Pick a day, understand its fuel, log the next meal or activity, and tick a planned action. Weight and diet remain within reach.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact brand/settings, personal greeting, seven selectable dates, a cobalt energy ruler with real intake and remaining budget, and prominent meal/activity actions. Mobile is vertical; desktop divides fuel/actions from timeline/habits.

FORM: Candidate 7, daily digital ledger, seed fa3fb0ff. Code-led under the user's delegated direction. The roll ran degraded without challengers. Signature: the selected date glides between days while the energy ruler and real day data respond. Activity composition expands inline; counts and task state animate; reduced motion removes travel.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
