# Body weight history extension

Mode: Operate. Scope: replace the weight graph, retain surrounding Body tasks and saved data. Code-led local extension; no concept seed or identity replacement.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dated measurement chart, not a decorative sparkline: recorded weights and calendar-based averages must be distinguishable.

OWN-WORLD: Existing chalk/cobalt/Hanken world, one white bordered measurement panel, labeled axes, flat detail rows, 44px controls. No emojis or new global tokens.

STORY: Pick 7/30/90 days or all history, inspect a real weigh-in by touch or keyboard, compare with its trailing calendar-week average and saved goal. Missing days are not invented.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep Body header, weigh-in action and overview; place history immediately after overview, before goal/calorie scenarios. Selected date and separate measured/trend values lead the plot.

FORM: Runtime SVG measurement chart with proportional calendar spacing, a nonzero labeled weight scale, primary recorded-weight line and secondary dashed calendar-average line, both broken over long gaps. Signature: dragging the chart or using keyboard/previous-next updates one clear recorded value, with its average visually subordinate. Period lives in a compact header select; methodological copy and text history are collapsed. Sparse/empty states remain available. The first open composition and native range slider were rejected by the user as still ugly; this panel replaces that composition rather than tweaking its colors.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
